## How to Get Started

Getting Apple Pup Cup into your shop is simple:

1. **Reach out** — send us an email with your shop name and location
2. **We'll connect** — a quick call or message to discuss your needs and find the right product mix
3. **Place your first order** — we'll handle packaging, labeling, and delivery
4. **Start serving** — hand over your first pup cup and watch the smiles happen
